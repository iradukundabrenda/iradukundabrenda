# Hi, I'm Brenda Iradukunda

Senior at Washington and Lee University studying **Computer Science and Economics** (minor in Data Science). I'm from Rwanda.

I build software for people who depend on systems they can't easily check, like blind users relying on image descriptions or first-time entrepreneurs relying on financial tools. I'm especially interested in **human-computer interaction, trustworthy AI, and accessible design**.

### What I'm working on
- **[Accessible Alt-Text](https://github.com/iradukundabrenda/Accessible-Alt-Text)**: a Flask app and Chrome extension that lets users click any image and hear its description read aloud. It grew out of my 2024 research on how AI-generated alt text describes people. *Next: integrating an image-captioning model.*
